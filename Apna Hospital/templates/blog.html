When AI should say "go to the hospital"

A symptom checker that only ranks diseases can be dangerously confident. Apna Hospital pairs the model with a deterministic safety layer: a short list of red-flag symptoms always triggers an emergency banner, whatever the model thinks.

Why we show three answers, not one

Symptoms overlap. Showing the top three with scores, then asking the one question that best separates them, mirrors how clinicians reason and keeps users from fixating on a single label.

Catching a silent data bug

The treatment table we inherited was shifted by one row, so several conditions showed another condition's medicines. We audited every row and rebuilt it. Always read your data.
